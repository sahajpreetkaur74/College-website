About Us

Our story, mission and journey so far.

Who we are

Founded in 2001, Riverside Institute of Technology has grown from a single department into a full campus with engineering, computing, and management programs.

We believe students learn best by making things. Every course includes labs, projects and industry visits.

Our Mission

To give every student practical skills and the confidence to use them.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted college for technology and management.

Our Values

Honesty, curiosity, teamwork and respect for every learner.

Our Journey

  1. 2001 Institute founded with 120 students and one department.
  2. 2008 Electronics and Mechanical departments added.
  3. 2015 New library and computer labs opened.
  4. 2020 Management school launched with BBA and MBA.
  5. 2025 Crossed 5,000 students and 92% placement.

Message from the Principal

Education is not about marks alone. At Riverside, we want you to leave with skills, good habits and a curious mind. Come and see our campus; we would love to meet you.

Dr. S. K. Verma, Principal
