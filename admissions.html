How to apply

  1. Fill the enquiry form below or visit the admission office.
  2. Submit documents: 10th and 12th marksheets, ID proof and photographs.
  3. Attend counselling and pick your course.
  4. Pay the first-term fee to confirm your seat.

Frequently asked questions

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Students scoring above 90% get a fee waiver of up to 50%.

Is hostel accommodation compulsory?

No. Hostel rooms are optional and allotted on a first-come basis.

Does the college provide placement support?

Yes. Our placement cell runs training from the second year and invites 60+ companies every year.
